1. Introduction
Motivation & The Real-World Automotive Gap
Why official lab ratings fail to reflect real-world vehicle impact and impending European regulations.
1. Lab vs. Road Discrepancy
  • WLTP Underestimation: Official laboratory test cycles systematically underestimate real driving energy & fuel consumption by 15% to 30%.
  • Empirical ADAC EcoTest: Independent tests capture realistic mixed cycles with cold starts, air conditioning loads, and high-speed highway segments.
  • The Epistemic Gap: Fleet managers purchasing purely from OEM datasheets face severe budget overruns and carbon compliance penalties.
2. Euro 7 Paradigm Shift
  • Beyond Tailpipe CO2: As vehicle fleets electrify, non-exhaust emissions (brake dust and tyre abrasion) dominate overall particulate pollution.
  • Regulation (EU) 2024/1257: World's first standard setting strict brake PM10 limits: 3 mg/km for BEVs vs. 7 mg/km for ICE/HEVs.
  • Tyre Microplastics: Quantifies tyre abrasion rates (mg/km/t) and partitions 90% into water/soil runoff and 10% into breathable PM10.
3. Circular Battery Mandates
  • Regulation (EU) 2023/1542: Mandates digital Battery Passports tracking chemistry, capacity, and State of Health (SoH) durability.
  • Durability Milestones: Enforces legal guarantees of ≥80% SoH at 5 yrs / 100k km and ≥70% at 8 yrs / 160k km.
  • Residual Asset Value: Battery health dictates second-life stationary storage repurposing and vehicle depreciation.

1. Introduction
Why a Graph-Native Knowledge Graph?
Unifying multi-source testing data, regulatory standards, and supply-chain ontologies under formal semantics.
Limitations of Relational & Vector Approaches
  • Relational Data Silos: Tabular databases struggle with heterogeneous observations where testing protocols, vehicle variants, tyre fitments, and dynamic economic models share multi-tiered relationships.
  • Lack of Standard Semantics: Different OEMs and tyre manufacturers employ conflicting dimension formats and naming conventions, making relational joins brittle.
  • Vector Search Unreliability: High-dimensional embeddings lack verifiable constraint guarantees; they cannot prove that a fleet configuration complies with legal Euro 7 particulate limits or compute exact Pareto frontiers.
Knowledge Graph Value Proposition
  • Ontological Expressivity (OWL 2 DL): Formalizes vehicles, tyres, observations, battery milestones, and regulatory boundaries into a unified semantic graph.
  • Multi-Hop Traversal (SPARQL 1.1): Seamlessly traces vehicle → fitted tyre dimension → tyre abrasion wear rate → microplastic runoff → fleet TCO without rigid join tables.
  • Formal Validation (SHACL): Automated shapes validation enforces strict mathematical ranges and compliance limits across 29,739 RDF triples.
  • Industrial Standard Mapping: Aligned with COVESA VSS 4.0 signal paths and Catena-X Battery Pass specifications.

2. Knowledge Graph
Data Ingestion & Graph Construction Pipeline
Automated multi-source extraction, entity alignment, and provenance modeling across 28 source reports.
1. Multi-Source Ingestion
  • Automated Data Downloader: Custom fetcher (src/download_raw_data.py) validates SHA-256 hashes of all 28 raw reports with automated retry logic.
  • 26 Vehicle EcoTests: High-precision PDF parser extracts 608 vehicle observations (WLTP lab ratings, ADAC test values, powertrain specs).
  • 160 Tyre Abrasion Tests: Tabular parser extracts wear rates (mg/km/t) and wet/dry safety ratings across 162 tyre models.
2. Alignment & Modeling
  • Entity Alignment: Deterministic dimension-matching engine connects 161 vehicle-tyre configuration candidate pairs.
  • W3C SOSA/SSN Provenance: Every observation explicitly reifies source document, page number, test protocol, and observation context.
  • Deterministic Normalization: Tyre dimensions (e.g., 225/40 R18 vs 225/40ZR18) normalized into canonical semantic URIs.
3. Graph Scale & Metrics
  • 29,739 RDF Triples: Scaled graph (+1,811 triples over v1.0) serialized in Turtle syntax.
  • Granular Entities: 26 vehicles, 162 tyres, 631 vehicle observations, 1,170 tyre observations, 161 configurations.
  • SHACL Conformance: 100% conforming (0 errors, 0 violations) across all non-exhaust and battery constraints.

2. Knowledge Graph
Ontology & Schema Architecture
Extending AutoEcoKG with Euro 7 non-exhaust particulates, Catena-X Battery Pass, and COVESA VSS 4.0.
Core Ontological Classes & Properties
  • aekg:VehicleVariant — Tested models with powertrain type (BEV, HEV, PHEV, ICE) and curb weight.
  • aekg:TyreVariant — Tyres with width, aspect ratio, diameter, rolling resistance, and safety ratings.
  • aekg:TyreAbrasionObservation — Abrasion wear (mg/km/t), road runoff (90%), airborne PM10 (10%).
  • aekg:BrakeDustObservation — Brake wear emissions against Euro 7 limits (3 mg/km BEV, 7 mg/km ICE).
  • aekg:BatteryDurabilityObservation — SoH degradation milestones, battery chemistry (LFP, NMC), capacity.
  • aekg:VehicleTyreConfiguration — Synthesizes real-world fitments for holistic fleet decision-making.
Industrial Standards Alignment & Validation
  • COVESA VSS 4.0 Signal Paths: Classes include aekg:vssSignalPath mapping to in-vehicle telematics (e.g., Vehicle.Powertrain.TractionBattery.StateOfHealth).
  • Catena-X Battery Pass: Conforms to EU 2023/1542 digital product passport standards for circular economy traceability.
  • SHACL Shape Constraints: shacl_shapes.ttl validates tyre abrasion ranges (0–500 mg/km/t), brake dust bounds (≥0), and battery milestones.
  • Epistemic Reification: Separates official laboratory claims (WLTPProtocol) from empirical testing (ADACEcoTestProtocol).

3. Application
System Architecture & Core Analytical Modules
A modular three-tier pipeline connecting the RDF graph to real-time fleet intelligence.
Layer 1: Semantic Storage
  • RDFLib In-Memory Triplestore: Fast triplestore loading 29,739 triples from Turtle file in <1.5 seconds.
  • Ontology Reasoning: Applies RDFS class and property subsumption for dynamic querying.
  • SPARQL 1.1 Cache: Pre-compiled query templates for sub-millisecond analytical execution.
Layer 2: Analytical Core
  • Euro 7 Compliance Screener: Filters vehicle-tyre combinations against brake dust and tyre abrasion thresholds.
  • Fleet Microplastic Calculator: Models environmental fate (water runoff vs. airborne PM10) for arbitrary fleet sizes.
  • 5-Criterion Pareto Skyline: Implements non-dominated sorting across economic and environmental vectors.
Layer 3: Interactive UI
  • Streamlit Dashboard (app_v2.py): 12 dedicated interactive views for fleet managers and policy makers.
  • Interactive Parameter Sliders: Real-time adjustments for mileage, fuel/electricity tariffs, and carbon shadow taxes.
  • Visual Pareto Frontiers: Multi-dimensional Plotly scatter plots with particulate bubble sizing.

3. Application
5-Criterion Pareto Skyline Fleet TCO Engine
Multi-objective fleet optimization balancing economics, greenhouse gas, particulate wear, and safety.
5 Optimization Objectives
  • 1. 5-Year TCO (€): Acquisition + energy/fuel + maintenance + tyre replacements + CO2 shadow tax.
  • 2. WTW CO2 (g/km): Well-to-Wheel greenhouse gas emissions.
  • 3. Total PM10 (mg/km): Sum of exhaust, brake wear, and airborne tyre microplastics.
  • 4. Tyre Longevity (km): Service lifespan before replacement.
  • 5. Safety Score: ADAC driving safety and wet/dry braking.
Skyline Dominance Pruning
  • Zero-Weight Optimization: Eliminates subjective user weights; identifies mathematically non-dominated solutions.
  • Strict Dominance Rule: Config A dominates B if A is no worse in all 5 criteria and strictly superior in at least one.
  • Pruning Efficiency: Prunes 161 vehicle-tyre combinations down to ~12 Pareto-optimal fleet choices.
Actionable Fleet Impact
  • Dynamic Sensitivity: Real-time re-ranking as carbon price shifts from 0 to 300 €/tonne and electricity prices fluctuate.
  • Budget vs. CSR Alignment: Enables enterprise fleets to hit corporate ESG targets without inflating total capital expenditure.
  • SPARQL 1.1 Implementation: Fully implemented in queries/pareto_skyline_tco.rq.

4. Evaluation
Qualitative Evaluation & Automotive Discoveries
Domain discoveries and failure analysis surfaced by semantic graph queries.
1. Epistemic Divergence
  • Empirical Consumption Gaps: Heavy combustion models like Renault Espace (+26%) and Hyundai Tucson exhibit massive real-world fuel divergence above WLTP.
  • Thermal Penalty in BEVs: Electric vehicles reveal significant winter highway consumption increases due to cabin climate control and battery conditioning.
  • Procurement Value: Fleet operators relying on AutoEcoKG avoid unexpected operational budget deficits.
2. The BEV Mass Paradox
  • Regenerative Braking Win: Electric vehicles cut brake dust by >55%, easily beating the stringent 3 mg/km Euro 7 limit.
  • Tyre Abrasion Penalty: Heavier battery curb weights increase tyre abrasion wear by 12% to 18%, generating more microplastics.
  • The Solution: AutoEcoKG surfaces specific low-wear premium tyres (e.g., Michelin e.Primacy) that offset the mass penalty, achieving dual compliance.
3. Optimal Fleet Configurations
  • Urban / Regional Fleets: Compact BEVs paired with low-abrasion tyres dominate the Pareto frontier under moderate-to-high carbon taxation (>€80/t).
  • Highway Workhorses: High-efficiency diesels remain Pareto-optimal only where annual mileage exceeds 45,000 km in non-emission restricted zones.
  • Full Transparency: Every recommendation provides auditable provenance back to original ADAC test pages.

5. Conclusion & Reflection
Project Summary, Reflection & Future Outlook
Learnings from engineering AutoEcoKG 2.0 and future research trajectories.
Project Summary
  • Comprehensive Knowledge Graph: Unified 29,739 triples across vehicles, tyres, non-exhaust emissions, battery durability, and fleet TCO.
  • Regulatory Alignment: Operationalized Euro 7 (EU 2024/1257), Battery Pass (EU 2023/1542), and COVESA VSS 4.0.
  • Production-Grade Delivery: Streamlit interactive dashboard and 42/42 automated competency test suite.
Challenges & Reflection
  • Unstructured PDF Scraping: Minor layout shifts in ADAC reports across test years required resilient regex parsing strategies.
  • Microplastic Dispersion Granularity: Macro 90/10 environmental split is effective for fleet screening; localized runoff requires GeoSPARQL watershed modeling.
  • What We'd Do Differently: Adopt declarative R2RML / YARRRML mapping rules from the start to accelerate ingestion pipeline scalability.
Future Outlook
  • Live Telematics Streaming: Ingest real-time CAN-bus / OBD-II signals via COVESA VSS over MQTT/Kafka for real-time fleet wear tracking.
  • Catena-X Data Space Connector: Federated integration with OEM digital battery passports via Eclipse Dataspace Connector (EDC).
  • Graph Neural Networks: Train Link Prediction models on aekg:VehicleTyreConfiguration to predict real-world tyre lifespans on new unreleased vehicle models.
